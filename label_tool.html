NewsLens Labelling Desk

You are the teacher. Label real headlines, export labels.csv, and train your own tone classifier with train_tone.py.

NEUTRAL states what happened in plain words.
EMOTIONAL uses charged or dramatic wording that tries to make you feel something (shocking, devastating, soaring, terrifying).
OPINIONATED argues a view or gives advice: commentary, "why X is wrong", "you should".
Label the wording, not the news. Bad news reported calmly is NEUTRAL. A story you agree or disagree with doesn't change its label. Aim for 40+ of each label; more is better.

Press "Fetch headlines" to begin.

Keys: 1 neutral, 2 emotional, 3 opinionated, S skip, Z undo. Your work is saved in this browser, so you can label a little each day. Fetch again on another day to get new headlines.
